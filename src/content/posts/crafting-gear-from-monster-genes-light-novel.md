## A Gene-Based Power System Worth Your Time

If you've been craving a system fantasy light novel that goes beyond the usual stat-screen grind, **Crafting Gear From Monster Genes** offers a premise with a satisfying mechanical hook: in a world of Hunters, strength isn't simply leveled up — it's synthesized. Hunters harvest genes from the monsters they defeat and incorporate them into their own evolutionary path, gradually building something no one else can replicate.

The twist that makes this interesting is asymmetry. Not every gene is useful to every Hunter — your own trajectory determines what's worth pursuing, which forces real strategic thinking about which monsters to hunt and which traits actually fit your build. It's the kind of system that rewards readers who like optimization without drowning them in spreadsheet logic.

## Why the Premise Holds Up

The crafting angle implied by the title adds another layer: gear here isn't vendor-bought. It's forged from what the dangerous world around you provides, which ties character growth tightly to combat and exploration rather than treating equipment as an afterthought. The setting feels appropriately grim — power hierarchies among Hunters are real, alliances are fragile, and navigating contested zones requires as much reading-the-room as raw strength.

For readers who enjoy series like **Overgeared** — the long-running crafting-focused system fantasy manhwa with 317+ chapters that remains one of the strongest entries in the genre — this LN scratches a similar itch in prose form: a protagonist who wins through ingenuity and resource mastery as much as brute force.
