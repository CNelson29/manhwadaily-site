## Season 3 Is Over — Three Forbidden Masters Reaches Chapter 115

The word is spreading through fan communities: *I Belong To My Three Forbidden Masters* has wrapped its third season at chapter 115, marking the end of one of the more discussed forbidden-romance light novels in recent reading circles. If you have been following along, this is the moment readers knew was coming — and if you haven't started yet, there is now a complete arc ready to binge from chapter one.

The premise driving this series is a compelling one: a heroine entangled with not one but three masters, each relationship weighted with tension, unspoken rules, and the thrill of crossing lines that carry real consequences. Multi-love-interest forbidden romance is a proven formula in light novel fiction, and the fact that this series sustained reader interest through three full seasons speaks to how effectively it delivers on that premise. The emotional stakes of forbidden dynamics, layered across multiple love interests, give each chapter something to push against — and reaching a genuine ending after 115 chapters is a rarer achievement than it might seem.

Fan discussion boards confirm the finale has landed and readers are actively trading recommendations for what to pick up next. If you want to read or catch up on earlier chapters, the series is hosted at [FreeWebNovel](https://freewebnovel.com/novel/i-belong-to-my-three-forbidden-masters/chapter-58) — check the site directly for the full chapter listing including the finale.

## Still Ongoing: I Became My Master's Forbidden Love

When a beloved series ends, the natural question is: what scratches the same itch? If the master-disciple tension and forbidden-romance momentum of *Three Forbidden Masters* is what kept you hooked, *I Became My Master's Forbidden Love* is worth moving to your reading list immediately.

The story orbits a rebellious disciple and their master — a dynamic built on push-pull tension, promises made and broken, and the kind of charged back-and-forth that defines the best romance light novels in this genre. The setup is deceptively simple but the emotional texture it creates chapter after chapter is what keeps readers coming back. With over 140 chapters already available and new updates dropping regularly, there is plenty to sink into right now rather than waiting for a series to finish.
