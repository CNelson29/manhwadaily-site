## Spotlight: Trash Artisan: Crafting God-Tier Gear From Garbage

**Author:** Youssef_Tiichoo  |  **Genre:** Fantasy, Action, System  |  **Status:** Ongoing

Kael walked into Oakhaven Academy's Awakening Ceremony expecting his life to change — and it did, just not in the direction anyone wanted. While his classmates unlocked prestigious combat-ready abilities like Iron Knight and Elementalist, Kael received something far less celebrated: an F-Rank class built entirely around working with discarded scraps and refuse. In a world where your class determines your social standing, being handed *Trash Crafting* is about as low a starting point as the system allows.

That premise is precisely what makes the novel compelling. The crafting-focused power set is a genuine departure from the combat classes that dominate system fantasy, and the central question the story keeps raising — what happens when someone takes the one thing nobody values and makes it into something nobody saw coming — is satisfying to follow chapter by chapter. The academy hierarchy and social underdog framing will feel familiar to readers who enjoy titles like *Solo Leveling*, but the salvage-and-craft angle gives *Trash Artisan* its own distinct personality within the genre.

With an active release schedule and a chapter count that keeps growing, now is a good time to start from the beginning and catch up. Readers who enjoy watching a protagonist grind upward from a genuinely disadvantaged position, and who like crafting systems alongside (rather than instead of) action, should find plenty to enjoy here.
