## Introduction

Solo Leveling set the gold standard for action manhwa — a breathtaking art style, an incredibly satisfying power fantasy, and a protagonist whose glow-up is one of the greatest in fiction. But once you've watched Sung Jin-Woo ascend from E-Rank hunter to Shadow Monarch, where do you go next? Don't worry — we've got you covered. These 10 manhwa deliver the same rush of epic battles, world-threatening stakes, and jaw-dropping artwork that made Solo Leveling a global phenomenon.

## The Rankings

### 1. Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint

Kim Dokja is the sole reader of a web novel called 'Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse' — and then the world turns into that exact novel. Armed with future knowledge no one else possesses, he must guide humanity through apocalyptic scenarios while hiding just how much he knows. The art is dynamic and expressive, and the story layers meta-narrative brilliance over relentless, high-octane action across 180+ chapters.

**Why it's great:** The protagonist's strategic brilliance mirrors Jin-Woo's cool-headed dominance, and the emotional payoff in later arcs is absolutely devastating in the best way possible.

**Rating: 9.8/10**

### 2. The Beginning After the End

King Grey, the most powerful mage in his world, dies and is reborn as Arthur Leywin in a fantasy realm filled with magic and monsters. He carries the wisdom of a veteran ruler while growing into a strength that transcends his former life. With over 180 chapters and consistently gorgeous full-color artwork, this manhwa is an addictive blend of isekai, dungeon-crawling, and epic political intrigue.

**Why it's great:** The slow-burn power progression feels enormously earned, and the emotional core — Arthur's bonds with family and friends — gives the fights real weight.

**Rating: 9.5/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=The+Beginning+After+the+End) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=The+Beginning+After+the+End) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=The+Beginning+After+the+End)

### 3. Nano Machine

Cheon Yeo-Woon, a lowly illegitimate prince in a brutal martial arts world, receives a nanomachine implant from a mysterious descendant of the future — instantly transforming his potential. The manhwa blends wuxia martial arts with modern sci-fi upgrades in a combination that's wildly fun and endlessly creative across 180+ chapters. The action choreography is some of the best in the genre, with fluid panel layouts that make every fight feel cinematic.

**Why it's great:** If you loved Solo Leveling's RPG-style system upgrades, Nano Machine delivers that same dopamine hit wrapped in an elegant martial arts package.

**Rating: 9.3/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Nano+Machine) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Nano+Machine) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Nano+Machine)

🛒 [Get the Official English Volume on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Nano+Machine+manga+volume+1&tag=manhwadaily-20)

### 4. Overgeared

Grid, a hopeless loser in real life, stumbles upon the rarest blacksmithing class in the virtual reality MMORPG Satisfy and begins a slow, grind-fueled climb to becoming the most overpowered player in the world. With 200+ chapters of crafting, dungeon raiding, and increasingly insane boss fights, Overgeared is a love letter to gaming culture done right. The art evolves dramatically over its run, and Grid's character development from cringe-worthy to genuinely heroic is one of manhwa's best arcs.

**Why it's great:** The crafting-focused power system is unique and satisfying, and the sheer scale of the world — guilds, kingdoms, gods — keeps expanding in ways that always feel fresh.

**Rating: 9.1/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Overgeared) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Overgeared) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Overgeared)

### 5. Eleceed

Jiwoo is a kind-hearted young man with lightning-fast reflexes who discovers a secret world of ability users — and ends up housing a world-class operative disguised as a chubby cat. Created by the team behind Noblesse, Eleceed runs on Webtoon with 260+ chapters and features some of the most expressive, energetic art in modern manhwa. The balance between laugh-out-loud comedy and genuinely hype action sequences is pitch-perfect.

**Why it's great:** It captures the same underdog-to-powerhouse energy as Solo Leveling while adding heart and humor that makes the characters impossible not to love.

**Rating: 9.0/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Eleceed) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Eleceed) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Eleceed)

🛒 [Get the Official English Volume on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Eleceed+manga+volume+1&tag=manhwadaily-20)

### 6. Return of the Mount Hua Sect

Chungmyung, the greatest disciple of the legendary Mount Hua Sect, dies defeating the Demon Cult's leader — and is reborn 100 years later to find his sect in shambles. Armed with peak-level martial arts knowledge in a weak reincarnated body, he must drag his sect back to glory through sheer stubbornness and genius. The art style is rich and detailed with gorgeous ink-wash aesthetics, and the 170+ chapters are packed with standout fight sequences and brilliant comedic timing.

**Why it's great:** The gap between Chungmyung's true power and his perceived status creates endless satisfying moments where he demolishes expectations — pure power-fantasy gold.

**Rating: 9.2/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Return+of+the+Mount+Hua+Sect) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Return+of+the+Mount+Hua+Sect) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Return+of+the+Mount+Hua+Sect)

### 7. Second Life Ranker

Yeon-woo discovers his twin brother's hidden diary revealing he was betrayed and murdered inside a brutal dimensional tower — and vows to climb that same tower to take revenge. He inherits his brother's hidden class and legacy, unlocking powers that make him one of the most terrifyingly capable protagonists in manhwa. The dark tone, intricate lore, and relentless pacing across 170+ chapters make it a perfect successor to Solo Leveling's brooding intensity.

**Why it's great:** The revenge narrative gives every power-up genuine emotional stakes, and the tower's world-building is deep enough to lose yourself in completely.

**Rating: 9.0/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Second+Life+Ranker) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Second+Life+Ranker) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Second+Life+Ranker)

### 8. Player Who Returned 10,000 Years Later

Ko-Kun spent 10,000 years trapped in Hell, rising to become its absolute ruler — and then finds a way back to Earth, which is now suddenly infested with gates and monsters he could crush in his sleep. The sheer gap between his godlike power and the world's understanding of him makes for some of the most satisfying panels in recent manhwa memory. With a slick dark art style and rapid-fire action, this is essential reading for fans of overwhelmingly powerful protagonists.

**Why it's great:** If you ever wished Jin-Woo was even MORE overpowered, this manhwa answers that prayer while still finding ways to create tension and intrigue.

**Rating: 8.8/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Player+Who+Returned+10000+Years+Later) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Player+Who+Returned+10000+Years+Later) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Player+Who+Returned+10000+Years+Later)

### 9. The Max Level Hero Has Returned

Prince Davey O'Rowane, a weak and scorned royal, has his soul transported to a sanctuary where he trains alongside legendary heroes for a thousand years — returning to his original body as a max-level warrior in a world that still underestimates him. The art is bright, polished, and packed with expressive character work, and the 130+ chapters deliver consistent, crowd-pleasing action. The political scheming layered beneath the power fantasy adds satisfying depth.

**Why it's great:** The wish-fulfillment factor is off the charts, and watching Davey dismantle every noble who looked down on him never gets old.

**Rating: 8.7/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=The+Max+Level+Hero+Has+Returned) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=The+Max+Level+Hero+Has+Returned) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=The+Max+Level+Hero+Has+Returned)

### 10. Murim Login

Jin Tae-Kyung is a low-ranked hunter who discovers a VR device that transports him into a brutal murim (martial arts) world — and every skill and ability he earns there carries over to reality. The clever dual-world concept keeps the pacing snappy and inventive across 180+ chapters, while the art delivers both gorgeous martial arts sequences and tense modern-day dungeon raids. It's the perfect mashup of dungeon-hunter and wuxia genres.

**Why it's great:** The cross-world progression system scratches the same RPG-upgrade itch as Solo Leveling with a creative twist that keeps it feeling wholly original.

**Rating: 8.9/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Murim+Login) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Murim+Login) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Murim+Login)
