## Free Manhwa on Tapas: What You Actually Get

Tapas is the second-largest English manhwa platform after Webtoon, and it's home to some genuinely excellent series you can start reading right now without paying anything. The platform uses an "ink" system where the very latest episodes unlock early with premium currency, but the vast majority of older episodes are free for registered users — no subscription required.

The titles below range from record-breaking BL comedies to high-stakes action fantasies. Several are completed, meaning you can binge the entire run without waiting for new chapters. Note that platform availability can shift over time, so always verify directly on tapas.io.

## Rankings: Best Free Manhwa on Tapas

### 1. Solo Leveling — Our Score: 9.5/10

Ten years after mysterious gates connecting the human world to monster-filled dimensions appeared across Earth, certain people gained the power to fight back as hunters. Sung Jinwoo starts as the weakest hunter alive — until a near-death trial grants him a system that lets him level up without limit. This completed action manhwa is the benchmark against which everything else in the genre gets measured, and it's confirmed as one of Tapas' flagship free titles.

**Why it's great:** Tightly plotted from start to finish, with art that escalates alongside the protagonist's growing power. Being completed means you get full story payoff with no waiting.

### 2. The Beginning After the End — Our Score: 9.3/10

A powerful king reincarnates into a world of magic and monsters, retaining all his memories and experience while growing up again in a completely different life. TBATE built one of Tapas' largest loyal fanbases and is cited as one of the platform's top completed free series. It's a long-running manhwa with an intensely devoted readership.

**Why it's great:** The mix of isekai world-building, a genuinely competent protagonist, and emotional story beats sets it apart from typical overpowered fantasy. It's one of the defining manhwa of the Tapas platform.

Find it by searching "The Beginning After the End" directly on [tapas.io](https://tapas.io).

### 3. Moonshine — Our Score: 8.8/10

Moonshine is a BL comedy manhwa that has become one of Tapas' most-watched series, accumulating over 147 million views and 7.8 million likes on the platform. It blends LGBTQ+ themes with sharp comedy in a way that has clearly resonated with a massive global readership. If you want to understand why Tapas became a hub for BL manhwa, this is the place to start.

**Why it's great:** Those 147 million views aren't an accident — Moonshine delivers consistent charm chapter after chapter, and it's completely free to read on Tapas.

### 4. The Carefree Life of an SSS-Class Knight — Our Score: 8.5/10

An SSS-Class knight at the absolute peak of power who doesn't feel the need to prove it every chapter — this series offers a refreshing twist on the overpowered protagonist formula. It has racked up nearly a million views and over 67,000 likes on Tapas, which signals readers are finding exactly what they came for.

**Why it's great:** The contrast between overwhelming strength and a relaxed attitude creates natural comedy without sacrificing the action. Episode 1 is free and sets the tone immediately.

### 5. Past Life Returner — Our Score: 8.3/10

An action fantasy with over 450,000 views on Tapas, Past Life Returner follows a protagonist who gets a second chance after their previous life ends — armed with full knowledge of what's coming. Regression stories live or die on how the protagonist uses that foreknowledge, and this series' audience suggests it handles the balance well.

**Why it's great:** Clean execution of the regression formula with a protagonist who approaches their second life strategically rather than just breezing through everything unchallenged. Free to start on Tapas.

A regression manhwa with a compelling hook: the protagonist returns at absolute maximum power rather than having to grind their way back up. The Max Level Returner has a growing and dedicated audience on Tapas with thousands of likes and active subscribers on its series page.

### 7. Who Made Me a Princess — Our Score: 8.7/10

A modern woman reincarnates as Princess Athanasia from a romance novel she once read — except Athanasia's fate is to be executed by her own cold-hearted emperor father. Armed with knowledge of the story's plot, she must find a way to change that ending before it arrives. This reincarnation romance has been winning readers over since 2018 and holds up beautifully.

**Why it's great:** The emotional core — a child trying to earn genuine love from a father who sees her as an inconvenience — gives this manhwa surprising depth beneath its fantasy romance surface. Widely available across free manhwa reading platforms.

A modern girl reincarnates as Melissa, the villainess of a romance novel, and immediately decides the original story was badly written. Rather than playing the villain role, she sets about fixing everything — loudly, irreverently, and with zero patience for the male leads' nonsense. This completed series leans hard into self-aware comedy and subverts villainess tropes rather than playing them straight.

**Why it's great:** Completed run, sharp humor, and a protagonist with genuine agency rather than just reactive survival instincts. One of the freshest takes on the villainess isekai formula available on free manhwa platforms.
