## The Rankings

### 1. True Beauty

True Beauty follows Lim Jugyeong, a girl who transforms herself through the power of makeup after years of being bullied for her looks. When she meets the effortlessly cool Lee Suho at her new school, sparks fly — but so do secrets. With over 200 episodes and a hit K-drama adaptation, this is one of Webtoon's crown jewels. The art is absolutely stunning with incredibly detailed character designs, and the love triangle will keep you up until 3 AM debating teams. A must-read for any romance fan. **Rating: 9/10**

### 2. I Love Yoo

I Love Yoo centers on Shin-Ae Yoo, a fiercely independent girl with walls built sky-high, who gets entangled with two wealthy brothers in the most unexpected ways. Creator Quimchee masterfully blends humor, emotional depth, and slow-burn romance into every chapter. With a distinctive art style that balances crisp linework with expressive emotional storytelling, this series has built one of Webtoon's most dedicated fanbases and for very good reason. **Rating: 9.5/10**

**Where to read:** [Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=I+Love+Yoo) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=I+Love+Yoo) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=I+Love+Yoo)

### 3. Let's Play

Let's Play stars Sam Young, an indie game developer whose life gets hilariously complicated when the popular streamer Marshall Law moves in next door and unknowingly destroys her launch. What starts as a comedy of errors blossoms into one of the warmest, most genuine romances on the platform. With over 180 episodes, loveable supporting characters, and themes of social anxiety handled with real sensitivity, this series is an absolute gem. **Rating: 9/10**

**Where to read:** [Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Let%27s+Play) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Let%27s+Play) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Let%27s+Play)

### 4. Age Matters

Age Matters tackles the taboo of a romance between a successful 29-year-old woman, Rose Choi, and a gorgeous 23-year-old man, Daniel Yoon, as they navigate love, career pressure, and societal expectations. The art is lush and cinematic with an almost editorial fashion-magazine feel that makes every panel a treat. Creator Enjelicious packs incredible emotional punches alongside some truly swoon-worthy moments that will have you clutching your phone dramatically. **Rating: 8.5/10**

**Where to read:** [Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Age+Matters) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Age+Matters) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Age+Matters)

### 5. Lore Olympus

Lore Olympus reimagines the Greek myth of Hades and Persephone as a stunning, emotionally complex modern romance set among the gods of Olympus. Rachel Smythe's pastel-drenched watercolor art style is completely iconic and unlike anything else in the manhwa or webcomic space. With hundreds of episodes, a Harvey Award win, and a physical release that hit the New York Times bestseller list, this is Webtoon royalty in every sense of the word. **Rating: 9.5/10**

My Dear Cold-Blooded King is a lush historical romance following Kihara Mei, a simple merchant girl who catches the eye of the empire's most feared and enigmatic king. The intrigue, power dynamics, and slow-burn tension are executed brilliantly across its 130+ episodes. The detailed, richly colored art brings the imperial setting to life in a way that feels genuinely cinematic, and the chemistry between leads is absolutely electric. **Rating: 8.5/10**

Boyfriends is a short, sweet, and absolutely adorable slice-of-life romance following four very different boyfriends — the Prep, the Goth, the Nerd, and the Jock — who are all in a loving polyamorous relationship together. refrainbow's chibi-style art is impossibly cute and the series normalizes healthy, communicative relationships in a way that feels genuinely refreshing. Perfect for when you need something cozy and wholesome that will make you smile on a hard day. **Rating: 8/10**
