## Why Eleceed Fans Keep Searching for More

Eleceed hits a rare sweet spot: the supernatural action is kinetic and precise, the school setting keeps stakes personal, and the cat-powered premise delivers genuine comedy without undermining the fights. Finding that combination again is harder than it looks. The series below were chosen because each one shares at least two of Eleceed's core ingredients — hidden or escalating supernatural power, strong fight choreography, and a cast worth caring about.

Eleceed's anime adaptation was confirmed for 2026, and with Jiwoo and Kayden past the 400-chapter mark, the fandom is only growing. If you've caught up on the current chapters and need somewhere to go, this list covers the best next reads across Webtoon, MangaDex, and beyond.

## Rankings

### 1. Solo Leveling — Our Score: 9.6/10

The standard-setter for modern manhwa action. E-rank hunter Sung Jin-Woo gains a mysterious leveling system that lets him grow stronger in secret while the world around him stagnates. The art escalates alongside the power fantasy, delivering some of the most iconic monster-fight spreads in the genre.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** Both series center on a protagonist hiding their true strength while quietly becoming unstoppable. The pacing and the dopamine hit from power reveals feel almost identical.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/32d76d19-8a05-4db0-9fc2-e0b0648fe9d0)

### 2. The God of High School — Our Score: 9.4/10

A high-school martial arts tournament where competitors secretly channel the power of gods and mythological figures through a system called Charyeok. The fights start grounded and escalate into full-on divine confrontations with some of the most kinetic martial arts sequences in WEBTOON's catalog.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** The school tournament structure, hidden supernatural escalation, and comedy between brawls mirror Eleceed's tone almost exactly. Sources list this as the closest tone match in the genre for Eleceed fans.

[Search on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=The+God+of+High+School)

### 3. Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint — Our Score: 9.3/10

Office worker Dokja Kim spent years as the sole reader of a niche web novel — then the novel became real. His foreknowledge of every plot event makes him the most dangerous person in an apocalyptic world, even if no one believes him. The story balances massive supernatural set pieces with surprisingly emotional character work.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** The clever protagonist using hidden knowledge as a power mirrors Eleceed's theme of unseen strength, and the action only gets bigger as the story matures.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/9a414441-bbad-43f1-a3a7-dc262ca790a3)

### 4. UnOrdinary — Our Score: 9.0/10

In a world where supernatural abilities define your social rank, John attends a high school where the powerful bully the weak — and quietly conceals an ability that outranks everyone. The tension between his hidden power and his choice not to use it drives one of the most psychologically intense school-action stories on Webtoon.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** School setting, hidden overwhelming power, and the moral weight of when to fight back — UnOrdinary shares Eleceed's structural DNA at every level.

[Search on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=UnOrdinary)

### 5. Tower of God — Our Score: 8.9/10

A boy named Bam enters a mysterious tower to follow his only companion, Rachel, climbing floors filled with lethal tests, complex politics, and characters wielding god-tier supernatural abilities. The mythology deepens with every arc, and the Workshop Battle arc features the densest action choreography in the genre.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** Escalating supernatural stakes, a fiercely loyal protagonist, and fight scenes that reward close reading. Multiple sources specifically flag Tower of God for its action art quality relative to Eleceed.

[Search on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Tower+of+God)

### 6. SSS-Class Revival Hunter — Our Score: 8.7/10

Gongja Kim's one supernatural ability is copying other hunters' skills — useful in theory, useless in practice until the top-ranked hunter kills him for it. He wakes up in the past with both his copy skill and the killer's own time-reversal ability, setting off a methodical revenge arc built on layered power systems.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** The slow build of hidden power leading to devastating reveals mirrors Eleceed's satisfying payoff structure, and the psychological chess game adds an extra layer of tension.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/4a973243-952e-44d7-a50f-883b4b7c9cc2)

### 7. Second Life Ranker — Our Score: 8.5/10

After learning his twin brother was betrayed and murdered inside a secret tower competition, Yeonwoo enters the Obelisk armed with his brother's hidden journal and cold determination for revenge. The power system is intricate, the dungeon-crawling is visceral, and the personal motivation keeps every fight grounded.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** High-stakes supernatural combat with a deeply personal motivation — the same formula that makes Eleceed's fights matter beyond the choreography alone.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/1ffca916-3ad7-46d2-9591-a9b39e639971)

### 8. A Returner's Magic Should Be Special — Our Score: 8.3/10

Desir Arman is one of six humans to survive the Shadow Labyrinth, humanity's final and failed stand against extinction — then wakes up 13 years in the past at the magic academy where it all began. Every decision carries the weight of a future he is desperate not to repeat.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** The magic academy setting echoes Eleceed's school-action energy, and the protagonist's hidden foreknowledge creates the same dramatic tension as Jiwoo's concealed cat-speed reflexes.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/6a468761-5bd6-4de0-a0cb-47cb456ac2e0)

### 9. The Max Level Hero Strikes Back — Our Score: 8.1/10

Powerless Prince Davey is struck by an arrow and falls into a coma; his soul drifts to the Hall of Heroes, where the greatest warriors in history spend a thousand years training him. He returns to his body outwardly unchanged but secretly the most dangerous person in the kingdom.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** The gap between perceived weakness and actual overwhelming power is the exact dynamic Eleceed runs on, and the payoff when the prince finally cuts loose delivers the same satisfying reveal.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/b407de00-75a5-415a-a001-585fb41b9cf2)

### 10. The Skeleton Soldier Failed to Defend the Dungeon — Our Score: 8.0/10

A skeleton soldier who exists only to protect its master Lady Succubus watches her die, then resets — and resets, and resets — learning the dungeon's brutal secrets across each loop. The supernatural action is grounded in grief and stubborn loyalty rather than power fantasy, giving it emotional texture rare in the genre.

**Why it works for Eleceed fans:** The found-family loyalty at Eleceed's heart is replicated here in miniature — a being with minimal status who refuses to stop fighting for the person they care about.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/d993f789-e7e5-4832-92fd-37614220b427)
