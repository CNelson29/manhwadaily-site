## The Anime Ended — Here Is Where to Keep Reading

Solo Leveling's anime has done something rare: it made millions of viewers genuinely care about a manhwa protagonist. When Season 2 closed just before the Ahjin Guild arc, the demand for "where do I continue?" spiked immediately. The answer is straightforward, but the format choice matters, so this guide walks you through every official option and the exact chapter that picks up where the anime left off.

## Official Sources to Read Solo Leveling

There are two primary official channels in English:

- **Webtoon (webtoons.com)** — The main official English platform for the manhwa. You can read earlier chapters free on a rotating schedule, or use Webtoon coins for same-day access. Search: [Solo Leveling on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Solo+Leveling).
- **Yen Press** — Publishes the English light novel (the original source by Chu-Gong) and the manhwa in print volumes. The best option if you prefer physical books or want the most detailed version of the story.
- **MangaDex** — Community reference page: [Solo Leveling on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/32d76d19-8a05-4db0-9fc2-e0b0648fe9d0).

## Which Chapter to Start After Each Season

**After Season 1:** The first season covers through the Job Change arc and wraps up around **Chapter 45**. Jump into **Chapter 46** (the Red Gate arc) to continue without missing a beat.

**After Season 2:** Season 2 concluded just before the Ahjin Guild arc, leaving off after the Jeju Island Raid. Pick up from **Chapter 125** to continue Sung Jinwoo's story in the manhwa exactly where the anime stopped.

## Manhwa vs. Novel: Which Format Is Right for You?

The manhwa (illustrated by DUBU/Jang Sung-rak) is the most popular choice for anime viewers because its visual style matches the show most closely. The original light novel by Chu-Gong is the source material and goes into greater depth in certain scenes — worth reading if you want every detail. Most fans start with the manhwa and then explore the novel for context. Both are legitimate official adaptations.

## Solo Leveling: Ragnarok — The Sequel

If you finish Solo Leveling and want more of the same universe, *Solo Leveling: Ragnarok* is the official sequel manhwa, available in English via official channels. It follows a new protagonist in the same world. Search it by name on Webtoon or check comingsoon.net's guide for current availability details.

## 8 Manhwa to Read After You Finish Solo Leveling

Finished the manhwa and still not satisfied? These series from our reading list deliver the same power-progression highs, RPG-style systems, and high-stakes action that make Solo Leveling so addictive.

### #1 — Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint

An office worker who spent years reading a web novel suddenly finds the apocalyptic story becoming real — and he is the only one who knows how it ends. The narrative intelligence here is a step above most action manhwa: the protagonist does not just level up, he outmaneuvers a collapsing reality using plot knowledge as his weapon. The art scale during major battles is genuinely impressive. **Our score: 9.4/10.** [Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/9a414441-bbad-43f1-a3a7-dc262ca790a3) | [Read on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Omniscient+Reader%27s+Viewpoint)

### #2 — SSS-Class Revival Hunter

Gongja Kim has a copy ability that lets him steal one skill per death — and in a tower full of hunters who can kill him on a whim, that is both his curse and his path to becoming the strongest. The time-loop mechanics give it a psychological edge most action manhwa skip entirely. If you liked watching Jinwoo grind from the absolute bottom, this series scratches the exact same itch. **Our score: 9.1/10.** [Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/4a973243-952e-44d7-a50f-883b4b7c9cc2) | [Read on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=SSS-Class+Revival+Hunter)

### #3 — A Returner's Magic Should Be Special

One of six survivors of humanity's last catastrophe travels back to his academy days with full knowledge of every disaster to come — and the tactical intelligence to prevent them. The magic system is structured and consistent, and the protagonist's competence never feels unearned because we see exactly how he builds it. Completed, so you can binge the entire arc without waiting. **Our score: 8.9/10.** [Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/6a468761-5bd6-4de0-a0cb-47cb456ac2e0) | [Read on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=A+Returner%27s+Magic+Should+Be+Special)

### #4 — Second Life Ranker

Yeonwoo discovers his twin brother was betrayed and killed inside a deadly tower — and inherits a pocket watch containing everything his brother learned before dying. The revenge arc is deeply satisfying, and the tower's faction politics add layers that keep it from being a straight power fantasy. The art during boss fights is some of the best in this genre. **Our score: 8.7/10.** [Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/1ffca916-3ad7-46d2-9591-a9b39e639971) | [Read on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Second+Life+Ranker)

### #5 — The Max Level Hero Strikes Back

A powerless prince falls into a coma and his soul spends years training with legendary heroes before returning to a body everyone wrote off. The wish-fulfillment here is deliberate and unapologetic — and executed well enough that it works. Great pick if you want the satisfaction of an underestimated protagonist proving everyone wrong at scale. **Our score: 8.5/10.** [Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/b407de00-75a5-415a-a001-585fb41b9cf2) | [Read on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=The+Max+Level+Hero+Strikes+Back)

### #6 — The Skeleton Soldier Failed to Defend the Dungeon

A low-level skeleton guard watches his mistress get murdered, then wakes up at the beginning of his timeline with his memories intact and a determination to get strong enough to protect her. The loop mechanic is used inventively — each reset teaches the protagonist something new, and the emotional core is surprisingly affecting for a story about a skeleton. **Our score: 8.3/10.** [Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/d993f789-e7e5-4832-92fd-37614220b427) | [Read on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=The+Skeleton+Soldier+Failed+to+Defend+the+Dungeon)

### #7 — Solo Leveling: Ragnarok

The official sequel set in the same world as Solo Leveling, following a new protagonist navigating the aftermath of Sung Jinwoo's story. If you want to stay in the same universe with fresh stakes, this is the direct continuation. Available in English through official channels — search by title on Webtoon or check the Yen Press catalog. **Our score: 8.0/10** (ongoing — score will update as arcs complete). [Search on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Solo+Leveling+Ragnarok)

### #8 — Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint (Novel)

If you loved the manhwa adaptation of Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint, the original web novel goes significantly deeper into the protagonist's internal logic and the worldbuilding. Available via official translation channels for readers who want every scene the manhwa condensed. Think of it as the director's cut of an already excellent series. **Our score: 9.2/10.** [Check on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Omniscient+Reader%27s+Viewpoint+manhwa&tag=manhwadaily-20)
