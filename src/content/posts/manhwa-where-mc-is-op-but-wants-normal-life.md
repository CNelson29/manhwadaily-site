## Why “OP But Wants a Normal Life” Hits Different

There’s something deeply relatable about a character who could level mountains with a finger — and just wants to eat breakfast in peace. The “overpowered protagonist who craves normalcy” is one of manhwa’s most satisfying tropes: you get the power fantasy *and* the underdog heart. Whether it’s a former heavenly demon trying to dodge disciples, or an SSS-class knight desperately avoiding the next raid, these stories find comedy, tension, and genuine warmth in the gap between what a character *is* and what they *want to be*.

Below we’ve ranked the best manhwa that nail this premise — from titles that wear it right in the name to beloved classics where the “I just want peace” energy runs deep.

## Rankings

### 1. I Have an SSS-Rank Trait, but I Want a Normal Life — Our Score: 8.5/10

Yoo Sungwon is an ordinary man in a world swarming with supernatural beings called Celestials and the Awakened — and he wants absolutely nothing to do with any of it. His quiet life shatters when a mysterious text transforms him into a rare SSS-class knight gifted with abilities most hunters can only dream of. The series commits fully to the comedic tension of an extremely powerful person desperately trying to avoid becoming a hero.

**Why it’s great:** It leans hard into reluctant-power comedy while keeping the threat of the Celestials real enough that the stakes never feel hollow. The most literal take on the trope on this list.

[Read on Webtoon ↗](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=I+Have+an+SSS-Rank+Trait+but+I+Want+a+Normal+Life)

### 2. The Heavenly Demon Wants a Quiet Life — Our Score: 9.0/10

A martial arts legend at the absolute peak of power decides the cultivation world can sort itself out, because all he wants is to live quietly. This Webtoon action original plays the concept straight and for laughs in equal measure — an unstoppable fighter repeatedly dragged back into the spotlight by disciples, rival sects, and his own reputation.

**Why it’s great:** The comedy of scale — a man who could destroy armies, annoyed by minor inconveniences — never gets old, and the action sequences hit hard when they finally arrive. One of the most charming murim entries on this list.

[Read on Webtoon ↗](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=The+Heavenly+Demon+Wants+a+Quiet+Life)

### 3. The Genius Wants an Ordinary Life — Our Score: 8.5/10

A genius-level protagonist with abilities far beyond the norm tries — repeatedly and desperately — to live an unremarkable life. The series has built a dedicated following on Webtoon through its slice-of-life warmth and strong character work, with Season 2 actively releasing through mid-2026. The contrast between the MC’s extraordinary potential and their mundane goals drives both the comedy and the emotional beats.

**Why it’s great:** It’s gentler than the action-heavy entries here, making it a great pick if you want the “normal life” wish fulfilled more often than frustrated. Cozy, warm, and consistently satisfying.

[Read on Webtoon ↗](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=The+Genius+Wants+an+Ordinary+Life)

### 4. SSS-Class Revival Hunter — Our Score: 9.0/10

Before the power-up, Gongja Kim was living a mundane existence in the Tower, envying star hunters from the sidelines. When he copies the strongest hunter’s legendary revival ability — and then gets killed for it — he’s thrown into a brutal loop that forces him to climb from the absolute bottom. The irony is delicious: a man who just wanted an interesting life gets far more than he bargained for.

**Why it’s great:** Psychological depth and a creative revival mechanic set it apart from standard isekai climbers. Gongja’s arc from envious bystander to unstoppable force is one of manhwa’s best power-up journeys.

[Read on MangaDex ↗](https://mangadex.org/title/4a973243-952e-44d7-a50f-883b4b7c9cc2)

### 5. Solo Leveling — Our Score: 9.5/10

Sung Jinwoo starts as the weakest hunter in a world where people risk their lives in monster-filled Gates — dismissed, underpaid, and barely surviving. After a near-death encounter triggers a mysterious System, he levels up in secret, transforming into a being no power scale can accurately classify. The series that redefined the genre and the benchmark every OP MC manhwa is measured against.

**Why it’s great:** Spectacular art, perfectly paced escalation, and Jinwoo’s quiet desire to simply protect his family gives the spectacle genuine emotional grounding. Non-negotiable on any list like this.

[Read on MangaDex ↗](https://mangadex.org/title/32d76d19-8a05-4db0-9fc2-e0b0648fe9d0)

### 6. The Max Level Hero Strikes Back — Our Score: 8.0/10

Prince Davey O’Rowane was powerless and overlooked until an enemy’s arrow put him in a coma — and his soul spent that time training with a thousand legendary heroes. He wakes up at max level in a body everyone still assumes is weak. He doesn’t want war or recognition; he wants to be left alone, but the world keeps underestimating him.

**Why it’s great:** The gap between how others see Davey and what he can actually do generates endlessly satisfying power-reveal moments. A reliable, fun read for fans of the “hiding your strength” variation of the trope.

[Read on MangaDex ↗](https://mangadex.org/title/b407de00-75a5-415a-a001-585fb41b9cf2)

### 7. A Returner’s Magic Should Be Special — Our Score: 8.5/10

Desir Arman survives the apocalypse as one of humanity’s last six survivors, only to be sent back in time to when it all began. Armed with foreknowledge of every disaster, he enrolls in a magic academy and sets out to prevent catastrophe — while hiding just how extraordinary he truly is. The “normal life” angle is subtle, but the desire to protect loved ones without revealing his true power is the series’ emotional engine throughout.

**Why it’s great:** An inventive magic system, tight pacing, and global-scale stakes. Being completed is a genuine bonus — no waiting for an ending.

[Read on MangaDex ↗](https://mangadex.org/title/6a468761-5bd6-4de0-a0cb-47cb456ac2e0)

### 8. Second Life Ranker — Our Score: 8.0/10

Yeonwoo discovers his brother was betrayed and killed inside the brutal Tower, and sets out to climb it using the hidden legacy left behind. What begins as a grief-driven quest becomes a relentless ascent as he surpasses every expectation. The “I never asked for this power” undercurrent runs throughout even as the revenge plot takes centre stage.

**Why it’s great:** The brother’s backstory creates emotional weight that most action manhwa skip entirely, and the complex power system rewards readers who enjoy deep lore.

[Read on MangaDex ↗](https://mangadex.org/title/1ffca916-3ad7-46d2-9591-a9b39e639971)
