## Why Completed Manhwa Like Solo Leveling Are So Hard to Find

Solo Leveling changed what manhwa readers expect: a weak protagonist who earns his power through brutal dungeons, a world that escalates logically, and an ending that actually delivers. The problem is that most 'Solo Leveling-like' manhwa are still ongoing — meaning you catch up and wait months between chapters. This list focuses on series that are fully complete or have enough chapters to binge without interruption, ranked by how closely they capture the same energy.

We cross-checked completion status across MangaDex, Webtoon, and multiple 2026 recommendation sources. Some entries share Solo Leveling's dungeon-gate setting directly; others carry the same spirit — an underestimated protagonist who becomes unstoppable through will, a broken ability, or sheer grinding.

## Top Manhwa Like Solo Leveling — Completed Picks

### 1. Solo Leveling — The Benchmark

**Status: Completed (179 chapters)**

Ten years after the Gate connecting our world to a monster dimension opened, ordinary people awakened as hunters. Sung Jinwoo starts as humanity's absolute weakest — the only hunter incapable of leveling up — until a hidden dungeon rewrites everything. The power fantasy is pitch-perfect, and unlike most manhwa that fade in their final arc, Solo Leveling sticks its landing with one of the cleanest endings in the medium.

**Why it's great:** The dungeon artwork is among the best ever drawn for manhwa, and the pacing never wastes a chapter. If you haven't finished it, start here.

**Our score: 9.8/10**

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/32d76d19-8a05-4db0-9fc2-e0b0648fe9d0)

### 2. A Returner's Magic Should Be Special

**Status: Completed**

Desir Arman is one of six humans to survive the Shadow Labyrinth — the deadliest dungeon humanity has ever faced. Then he's sent back in time to before it all began, with full knowledge of every trial ahead. The magic academy setting gives it a different flavor from Solo Leveling, but the dungeon-crawling tension, escalating power system, and 'one man who knows more than everyone else' dynamic will feel immediately familiar.

**Why it's great:** A rare manhwa where 'already knows everything' is used to build drama rather than eliminate it. The final arc earns its conclusion. One of the few truly completed action manhwa in this genre.

**Our score: 8.5/10**

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/6a468761-5bd6-4de0-a0cb-47cb456ac2e0)

### 3. SSS-Class Revival Hunter

**Status: Ongoing**

Gongja Kim lives in the RPG-like Tower envying the star hunters ranked above him — until he copies the most terrifying ability imaginable: die, steal the killer's power, and reset to one day before. What starts as a punishment loop becomes the most efficient leveling system in the Tower. If you loved Solo Leveling's 'weakest becomes strongest through a broken skill' arc, this is the closest structural match currently available.

**Why it's great:** The mechanic is simple on paper but the manhwa keeps finding new and psychologically gripping ways to apply it. Essential reading for Solo Leveling fans.

**Our score: 9.0/10**

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/4a973243-952e-44d7-a50f-883b4b7c9cc2)

### 4. Second Life Ranker

**Status: Ongoing (Season 4 returned 2026)**

Yeonwoo's brother disappeared, and he eventually learns the truth: his brother was betrayed and killed inside the Tower by his own teammates. Armed with his dead brother's hidden journal as a cheat sheet, Yeonwoo enters the same Tower and methodically dismantles everyone responsible. The Tower's floor-by-floor dungeon structure scratches exactly the same itch as Solo Leveling's gate system, with a revenge motivation that gives it real emotional grounding.

**Why it's great:** The brother-revenge arc hits harder than most pure power-fantasy setups. Multiple seasons of content mean there's plenty to binge before you catch up.

**Our score: 8.6/10**

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/1ffca916-3ad7-46d2-9591-a9b39e639971)

### 5. Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint

**Status: Ongoing (multiple awards)**

Dokja was an average office worker whose only hobby was reading a web novel called 'Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse.' When the novel's scenario suddenly becomes reality, he is the only person alive who knows how the story ends. The apocalypse unfolds with a full stat and skill system, monster invasions, and escalating dungeon-like scenarios — but filtered through a meta-narrative that makes it significantly smarter than the average system manhwa.

**Why it's great:** Consistently ranked alongside Solo Leveling as one of the best manhwa ever written. The character work and long-term payoff are extraordinary.

**Our score: 9.4/10**

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/9a414441-bbad-43f1-a3a7-dc262ca790a3)

### 6. The Skeleton Soldier Failed to Defend the Dungeon

**Status: Ongoing**

A skeleton soldier devoted to protecting his master fails — she is killed, and he is destroyed with her. He wakes up at the very beginning with all his memories intact. Each cycle he grows stronger, unlocking new skills through repeated deaths and dungeon runs, gradually building toward a power level that can actually change the outcome. The monster-world dungeon setting and relentless progression loop make this one of the most mechanically Solo Leveling-adjacent titles available.

**Why it's great:** The time-loop adds strategic depth that pure power manhwa usually skip. Every death has consequence and meaning.

**Our score: 8.3/10**

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/d993f789-e7e5-4832-92fd-37614220b427)

### 7. The Max Level Hero Strikes Back

**Status: Ongoing**

Prince Davey O'Rowane was powerless and universally dismissed as useless — until an enemy arrow sends him into a coma. His soul enters the Hall of Heroes, where history's greatest warriors train him for what feels like decades. He wakes up with god-tier skills hidden inside a frail prince's body, and the nobles who looked down on him are about to learn a hard lesson. The 'secretly overpowered' setup is pure Solo Leveling energy executed with confidence.

**Why it's great:** The wish-fulfillment is unapologetic and smartly executed. A reliable binge when you want spectacle without complexity.

**Our score: 8.0/10**

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/b407de00-75a5-415a-a001-585fb41b9cf2)

### 8. The God of High School

**Status: Completed (569 chapters)**

A massive martial arts tournament draws the best high school fighters from across Korea, promising to grant any wish to the winner. What starts as a straightforward tournament manhwa evolves into a full supernatural epic with divine powers, secret organizations, and world-ending stakes. It is longer than Solo Leveling but the action sequences rival anything in the medium, and it actually ends — a rarity at this scale.

**Why it's great:** Completed at 569 chapters with a definitive conclusion. The anime adaptation gave it global reach, but the manhwa goes deeper and hits harder.

**Our score: 8.7/10**

[Read on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=The+God+of+High+School)

### 9. Weak Hero

**Status: Completed (268 chapters)**

Gray Yeon is physically unimpressive and new to a school run by violent delinquents. But his tactical intelligence, pain tolerance, and cold precision make him a nightmare for anyone who underestimates him. Weak Hero is not a dungeon manhwa, but it captures Solo Leveling's core appeal completely: a protagonist written off by everyone around him who earns respect through sheer, terrifying will. The fight choreography is among the best in the genre.

**Why it's great:** One of the rare completed manhwa in the 'underestimated protagonist dominates' genre. Dark, grounded, and deeply satisfying from start to finish.

**Our score: 9.1/10**

[Read on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Weak+Hero)

### 10. Unholy Blood

**Status: Completed (92 chapters)**

Vampires have overrun the world, and only one half-vampire with extraordinary power stands between humanity and extinction. Hayan is trying to live quietly — until violence finds her again. Unholy Blood is tightly paced, completed in just 92 chapters, and delivers the same adrenaline that Solo Leveling provides without asking for a years-long time investment. The action choreography is creative and genuinely brutal.

**Why it's great:** Short, complete, and punchy. If you want a finished story with great fights and an overpowered female protagonist, this is the cleanest recommendation on this list.

**Our score: 8.2/10**

[Read on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Unholy+Blood)
