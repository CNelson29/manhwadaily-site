## Marvel Sorcery Light Novels Are Having a Moment

If you enjoy stories that transplant Marvel's mystical power structures into cultivation or isekai-style narratives, 2026 has been quietly generous to the genre. Fan-translated light novels built around sorcerers, reincarnators, and the Sorcerer Supreme throne have been releasing chapters at a steady pace — and this week at least two of them crossed a notable milestone. Here is where things stand.

## Sorcerer in the Marvel Universe — Chapter 465: Found the Target

Chapter 465 of *Sorcerer in the Marvel Universe* (hosted on Webnovel) just landed, carrying the title **Found the Target**. The story places its protagonist inside Marvel's layered magical hierarchy, and this latest chapter continues an arc that puts real weight on the Sorcerer Supreme's circle — including what it actually means to take on an apprentice and pass down that kind of power. If you have been following the threads involving Solomon and the mentorship dynamic in recent updates, this is the chapter fans of those plot lines have been anticipating.

The premise delivers exactly what the title promises: Marvel's mystical pecking order as the backdrop for a long-running power-progression story. It scratches the same itch as solo-levelling fantasies, but uses recognisable Marvel scaffolding to give the stakes extra texture. The chapter count alone — 465 and still climbing — is a reliable indicator of a story with a genuinely committed readership.

**Read at:** [Webnovel — Sorcerer in the Marvel Universe, Chapter 465](https://dynamic.webnovel.com/story/30711495308196305/85843780317528722)

## Into The Marvel Universe: A Reborn Sorcerer Novel

For a slightly different angle on the same core fantasy, *Into The Marvel Universe: A Reborn Sorcerer Novel* by Daoist_Kalyug_ deserves its own tab. The story follows Alex, someone the synopsis describes as a once-formidable wizard — the setup being a reincarnation or transmigration premise that drops a magic-user with serious prior experience directly into the Marvel universe and forces them to rebuild their footing from scratch against far more powerful opposition.

Community reception has been notably strong: the novel holds a 9.7 out of 10 from over three thousand reader ratings as of mid-2026, which is a high mark for this corner of the genre. The latest chapter as of May 2026 is Chapter 244, titled *Death's Gift*, placing the story firmly in its mid-arc with plenty of runway still ahead. If you prefer your Marvel fanfiction on the more polished end of the spectrum, this one is worth the investment.

**Read at:** [NovelBin — Into The Marvel Universe: A Reborn Sorcerer Novel](https://www.novelbin.cc/book/into-the-marvel-universe-a-reborn-sorcerer-novel/)

## Marvel's Master of Heavenly Magic — Also at Chapter 465

*Marvel's Master of Heavenly Magic* is the third title in this cluster worth flagging. It also just crossed the Chapter 465 mark, with an update titled **No More Pretending, It's All Out Now** — a title that suggests a long-building confrontation is finally being paid off in full. The author maintains a Patreon (patreon.com/Draco_) offering early access to chapters ahead of the main release schedule, a reliable signal of an active, paying readership keeping the story going. Chapters are available through aggregator mirrors; the link below points to the Chapter 465 entry.
