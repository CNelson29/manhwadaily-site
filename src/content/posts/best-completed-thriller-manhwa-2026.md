## The Best Completed Thriller Manhwa You Can Binge Right Now

Finding a completed thriller manhwa is rare. Finding one that sticks the landing — psychologically coherent, paced to hold tension, and finished without a dropped ending — is rarer still. This list focuses exclusively on completed series (or complete seasons), so you can read start-to-finish today with no waiting for updates.

According to reader consensus and critical tracking, **Bastard** consistently ranks as the gold standard: 92 chapters, completely finished, structurally tight from the first page. It is the benchmark everything else on this list gets measured against.

## Rankings

### #1 — Bastard

A high-school student discovers his charming, beloved father is a serial killer — and that he has been an unwilling participant in his crimes for years. At 92 chapters, Bastard is the most structurally tight completed thriller manhwa in the format. Every chapter earns its place. No filler, no padding, no wasted beats.

**Why it's great:** The psychological bind between the protagonist's terror of his father and his complicated loyalty is one of manhwa's finest constructions. The pacing is surgical and the conclusion is fully earned.

**Our score: 9.5/10.** The benchmark every other completed thriller manhwa gets measured against.

[Read Bastard on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Bastard)

### #2 — Sweet Home (Season 1)

After a reclusive teenager moves into a rundown apartment complex, the world outside collapses — humans begin mutating into monsters shaped by their deepest desires. Season 1 runs 141 chapters and tells a complete, self-contained story. The horror is visceral, but the psychological tension around who qualifies as human enough to save is where it really lives.

**Why it's great:** The ensemble cast's moral fractures are more frightening than the monsters. It is rare for survival horror manhwa to take its character psychology this seriously across a completed run.

**Our score: 9.2/10.** Essential reading if you can handle body horror alongside psychological tension.

[Read Sweet Home on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Sweet+Home)

### #3 — Killing Stalking (Mature)

One of Webtoon's most discussed and divisive completed series, Killing Stalking is a dark psychological thriller about obsession, captivity, and the extremes of human attachment. It is intense and mature — not a casual recommendation — but readers who engage with it on its own terms find a completed story that does not flinch from its own darkness.

**Why it's great:** Psychologically dense and deliberately uncomfortable, it explores territory most mainstream manhwa avoids entirely. The completed ending is fully committed to its premise.

**Our score: 8.8/10.** Essential for mature thriller fans with a high tolerance for dark subject matter.

[Read Killing Stalking on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Killing+Stalking)

### #4 — Dice

What if a mysterious die could rewrite your physical stats, appearance, and social standing — but entry into the game is controlled by a shadowy figure with his own hidden agenda? At 388 chapters, Dice is the longest completed entry on this list, and it earns every chapter with a slow-burn psychological unraveling of what people are willing to surrender for power.

**Why it's great:** The closest structural match to a game-theory thriller in manhwa form. The moral corruption that accumulates across its full run is handled with real craft and a satisfying resolution.

**Our score: 8.5/10.** Best for readers who want a longer, fully resolved psychological thriller arc.

[Read Dice on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Dice)

### #5 — Kill the Hero

The protagonist, betrayed and killed by the so-called hero he spent years helping reach the top, is reborn with his memories intact — and a clear, methodical plan for revenge. At 153 chapters, it is one of the more compact completed reads on Webtoon. Every scene is driven by the betrayal premise and the story delivers a proper conclusion.

**Why it's great:** The revenge structure is clean and the protagonist's deliberate approach to dismantling his enemy is satisfying in a way few manhwa manage across a full completed run.

**Our score: 8.2/10.** The fastest, most efficient entry point on this list for readers new to thriller manhwa.

[Read Kill the Hero on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Kill+the+Hero)

### #6 — Shotgun Boy

From the creator of Bastard and Sweet Home, Shotgun Boy is a completed survival-horror thriller set in a remote school where a teenager armed with a shotgun faces relentless supernatural threats. It is more intimate in scope than Sweet Home and tonally darker, which makes it feel tighter and more suffocating chapter-to-chapter.

**Why it's great:** The creator's signature ability to make confined spaces feel genuinely dangerous is fully on display. If you finished Bastard or Sweet Home, this is the natural next read.

**Our score: 8.0/10.** Essential for anyone working through the creator's completed catalogue in order.

[Read Shotgun Boy on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Shotgun+Boy)

### #7 — #Killstagram (Mature)

#Killstagram is a completed mature thriller about a stalker who films their obsession and posts the footage anonymously online. It weaponizes contemporary social media anxiety in a way that feels immediately threatening and uncomfortably familiar. The pacing is methodical and the payoff is earned rather than dropped.

**Why it's great:** The modern premise grounds the threat in recognizable, present-day fear rather than genre convention. One of the most contemporary-feeling completed thriller manhwa available.

**Our score: 7.8/10.** A strong pick for readers who want thriller rooted in a world that looks exactly like the one outside.

[Read #Killstagram on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Killstagram)

### #8 — Solo Leveling (Gateway Pick)

Solo Leveling is not a pure thriller, but its opening survival arc — an underpowered hunter trapped in a deadly dungeon with no exit — delivers genuine thriller tension in its best stretch. At 179 chapters, it is fully complete and one of the most culturally significant manhwa ever published in any genre. If you are new to manhwa, this is the standard entry point before moving into dedicated thriller territory.

**Why it's great:** The sheer momentum of its completed story is unmatched. An essential finished manhwa that every reader building a list should experience before branching into darker material.

**Our score: 9.0/10.** Not a thriller first, but the best gateway completed manhwa for readers just arriving at the genre.
