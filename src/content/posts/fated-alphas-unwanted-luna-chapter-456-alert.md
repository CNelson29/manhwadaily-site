## Spotlight: Fated: The Alpha's Unwanted Luna

The premise does a clever flip on a familiar setup. Viola enters the moment that's supposed to define her entire future — the day her wolf manifests and seals her place at the top of pack hierarchy. Instead, that moment becomes the beginning of her unraveling. She had carried herself as someone untouchable, spoken of alongside the Alpha heir, treated as a given at the center of her world. Then everything shifts, and she becomes exactly what the title says: unwanted.

What gives the story its pull is that tension between Viola's former reputation and what she now has to survive. She isn't a clean-slate underdog — she's someone who carried a "villain" label in other people's stories before her own story even had a chance to start. That layered starting point gives the romance and the pack politics actual dramatic weight instead of serving as pure backdrop.
