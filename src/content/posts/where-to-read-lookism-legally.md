## Where to Read Lookism Legally in 2026

The only official way to read Lookism in English is **WEBTOON**. The full series — 610+ episodes and still ongoing — is free to read at [webtoons.com](https://www.webtoons.com/en/drama/lookism/list?title_no=1049) with no subscription required. You can start at Chapter 1 right now at no cost. The WEBTOON app on iOS and Android is the smoothest reading experience, with offline downloads available for fast-pass episodes.

For Korean readers, Naver Webtoon hosts the original series. Print fans can find collected volumes on Amazon. Avoid any site that is not WEBTOON, Naver, or an officially licensed partner — most third-party readers are unlicensed and do not pay the creator.

## Lookism on Netflix: The Anime

Lookism also has an anime adaptation streaming on Netflix. The anime covers the opening arc of the manhwa — roughly chapters 1 through 27 — making it a low-commitment entry point if you prefer animation. Most fans who watch it end up returning to the manhwa to continue past where the anime ends.

## 8 Manhwa to Read After Lookism

Lookism sits at the intersection of social drama and underground action. The eight picks below share its underdog appeal, escalating fights, and power-shift storytelling.

### 1. Lookism — Our Score: 9.3/10

Park Hyung-Seok is an overweight loner who wakes up in a second, handsome body. During the day he attends school as his new self; at night his original body takes over. What begins as a story about appearance and social hierarchies at school grows into a sprawling saga of underground fighting guilds and criminal organizations. Over 610 episodes in and still ongoing, Lookism rewards patient readers with one of manhwa's most ambitious worldbuilding arcs.

**Why it's great:** No manhwa handles the gap between appearance and identity with this much depth. The escalation from school brawls to high-stakes underground politics is earned, not rushed.

[Read free on WEBTOON](https://www.webtoons.com/en/drama/lookism/list?title_no=1049)

### 2. Solo Leveling — Our Score: 9.5/10

Sung Jinwoo is the weakest hunter alive in a world where humans combat monsters through dimensional gates. After a near-fatal dungeon encounter, he alone receives a mysterious system that lets him level up in ways nobody else can. Completed at around 179 main chapters, it delivers a full and satisfying arc with some of the most dynamic art in the medium.

**Why it's great:** Like Lookism, it is fundamentally a story about someone at the bottom of a rigid hierarchy clawing their way to the top. The underdog momentum hits the same way, even in a completely different setting.

[Find on WEBTOON](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Solo+Leveling)

### 3. Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint — Our Score: 9.1/10

Kim Dokja is an ordinary office worker whose only hobby is following a web novel called *Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse*. When the novel's plot suddenly becomes reality, he is the only person who knows how the story unfolds — and how it ends. Ongoing and award-winning, this one earns every accolade it has received.

**Why it's great:** Fans of Lookism's layered storytelling will find the same satisfaction in watching a meticulous protagonist outsmart every scenario the world throws at him. Both series reward readers who pay attention to details planted chapters earlier.

[Read free on WEBTOON](https://www.webtoons.com/en/action/omniscient-reader/list?title_no=2154)

### 4. SSS-Class Revival Hunter — Our Score: 8.7/10

Gongja Kim lives inside a mysterious tower full of ranked hunters, perpetually envying the stars at the top. When he acquires the power to copy others' skills — and dies activating it — he resets to an earlier point in time with all his memories and hard-won knowledge intact. The loop mechanic gives this series a relentless forward momentum.

**Why it's great:** The tower's rigid hierarchy and the protagonist's drive to climb it echo Lookism's underground ranking system. Both worlds operate like ruthless games where your rank determines everything.

[Read on Tapas](https://tapas.io/series/sss-class-revival-hunter/info)

### 5. Second Life Ranker — Our Score: 8.4/10

Yeonwoo discovers that his long-missing brother died inside a hidden tower after being betrayed by his own party. Armed with his brother's secret journal, Yeonwoo enters the same tower to avenge him and uncover the full truth. Second Life Ranker builds one of manhwa's most satisfying revenge arcs through a well-constructed tower system.

**Why it's great:** The personal stakes and the slow revelation of conspiracy echo Lookism's own layered reveals about who controls the underground world. Both series reward patient readers who stick with the long game.

[Read on Tapas](https://tapas.io/series/second-life-ranker/info)

### 6. A Returner's Magic Should Be Special — Our Score: 8.2/10

Desir Arman is one of six humans to survive humanity's final catastrophe — the Shadow Labyrinth. Sent back in time to years before it happens, he has one mission: train the people around him to be strong enough to change the outcome. Completed, with an ending that wraps up its central premise cleanly.

**Why it's great:** Like Lookism, the core tension is about using superior knowledge to navigate a world built on visible power and rank. The magic academy setting gives it a distinct flavor from other time-travel action series.

[Find on WEBTOON](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=A+Returner%27s+Magic+Should+Be+Special)

### 7. The Skeleton Soldier Failed to Defend the Dungeon — Our Score: 7.9/10

A skeleton soldier loyally guards his master until they are both killed — and then resets, waking at an earlier moment with all his memories intact. Each death teaches him something new, and each reset gives him another chance to protect what he lost. The series does something rare: it makes you genuinely care about an undead side character.

**Why it's great:** The loop mechanic and gradual power accumulation mirror how Lookism's protagonist turns every setback into growth. Both series reward readers who appreciate slow, earned progression over instant power spikes.

[Find on WEBTOON](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=The+Skeleton+Soldier+Failed+to+Defend+the+Dungeon)

### 8. The Max Level Hero Strikes Back — Our Score: 7.7/10

Prince Davey O'Rowane is a powerless noble who falls into a coma — and inside it, his soul spends a thousand years training with the greatest heroes in history. He wakes up ordinary on the outside but with max-level abilities no one suspects. The contrast between his hidden strength and the contempt those around him show drives the story.

**Why it's great:** The everyone-underestimates-the-protagonist dynamic is the closest equivalent here to Lookism's dual-identity premise. The slow reveal of how powerful Davey actually is delivers the same deep satisfaction.

[Find on WEBTOON](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=The+Max+Level+Hero+Strikes+Back)
