## The OP MC + System Formula That Manhwa Does Best

System manhwa has a simple hook: an ordinary person gains access to a game-like interface — stats, skills, quests — and slowly (or explosively) becomes unstoppable. What separates the great series from the derivative ones is what they build on top of that premise: story, stakes, and a protagonist worth rooting for. The titles below are the ones that actually deliver. No padding, no filler — just eight series that genuinely earn their power fantasy.

## Rankings: Best Action Manhwa with OP MC and System Powers

### 1. Solo Leveling — Our Score: 9.5/10

Sung Jinwoo begins as the weakest hunter in a world where "Gates" connect reality to monster realms. A near-death encounter inside a double dungeon grants him a solo leveling system — the only hunter who can grow stronger indefinitely. What follows is one of the most satisfying power curves in all of manhwa: a slow first act, then a relentless escalation that rarely loses its sense of scale.

**Why it's great:** The art during boss fights is exceptional, and the world-building rewards patience. It's also complete — no waiting for a payoff that never arrives.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/32d76d19-8a05-4db0-9fc2-e0b0648fe9d0)

### 2. Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint — Our Score: 9.3/10

Kim Dokja is the only person who has read a web novel called "Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse" to its conclusion. When that novel becomes reality, his exclusive foreknowledge of the plot acts as a system-like advantage, letting him outmaneuver threats no one else even knows are coming. This is the most narratively ambitious entry on this list.

**Why it's great:** The system here is unconventional — it's knowledge, not a stat screen — which gives ORV a freshness that most system manhwa lack. The ensemble cast is large but never feels bloated.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/9a414441-bbad-43f1-a3a7-dc262ca790a3)

### 3. SSS-Class Revival Hunter — Our Score: 9.0/10

Gongja Kim is a low-ranked hunter stuck in a mysterious Tower, envying the top rankers above him. He gains the ability to copy the last skill used on him — and the first skill he copies is from the world's strongest: the power to return to the past upon death. What starts as a revenge setup becomes something far more layered.

**Why it's great:** The loop-and-revenge mechanic is used intelligently rather than lazily. Each reset advances the plot instead of resetting stakes, and the emotional beats land harder than expected for an action-first series.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/4a973243-952e-44d7-a50f-883b4b7c9cc2)

### 4. Second Life Ranker — Our Score: 8.8/10

Yeonwoo discovers his twin brother died inside a brutal hidden game — betrayed by his own party. Armed with his brother's diary and the items left behind, Yeonwoo enters the same Tower determined to climb to the top and exact revenge. The system here is layered, with skills, artifacts, and class unlocks feeding a satisfying progression loop.

**Why it's great:** The revenge motivation stays clean and consistent across hundreds of chapters, and the power scaling is aggressive enough to keep the action fresh without losing stakes.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/1ffca916-3ad7-46d2-9591-a9b39e639971)

### 5. Overgeared — Our Score: 8.7/10

Shin Youngwoo (known in-game as Greed) is a debt-ridden loser in the VR game "Satisfy" — until he stumbles onto a legendary item and unlocks the rare class "Pagma's Successor," a master blacksmith with serious combat potential. Instead of a classic stat-screen system, his power grows through crafting unique weapons no other player can obtain, making this one of the most creative takes on the OP MC premise in manhwa.

**Why it's great:** The game-world structure allows for constant novelty in power acquisition, and Greed's arc from bumbling underdog to calculated powerhouse is one of the best character progressions in the genre.

[Search on Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Overgeared)

### 6. The Max Level Hero Strikes Back — Our Score: 8.5/10

Prince Davey O'Rowane, powerless and dismissed by everyone at court, falls into a coma after being struck by an arrow. His soul travels to the Hall of Heroes, where the greatest warriors of all time train him across what feels like a lifetime. He returns carrying the skills of a thousand legendary fighters in a body that no one suspects. The gap between his appearance and his actual power drives most of the fun.

**Why it's great:** The "useless noble secretly maxed out" setup is executed with enough political intrigue layered on top to keep the between-fight chapters just as engaging as the combat.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/b407de00-75a5-415a-a001-585fb41b9cf2)

### 7. A Returner's Magic Should Be Special — Our Score: 8.3/10

Desir Arman survives humanity's final Shadow Labyrinth with only five others — then wakes up thirteen years in the past. With knowledge of every catastrophe to come and the magic skills he spent a lifetime developing, he re-enrolls in a magic academy to change history. His system is accumulated future knowledge, used with methodical precision.

**Why it's great:** Clean time-loop logic, consistently satisfying competency moments — the MC out-thinks enemies rather than just overpowering them — and a complete, finished arc make this one of the stronger series in the genre.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/6a468761-5bd6-4de0-a0cb-47cb456ac2e0)

### 8. The Skeleton Soldier Failed to Defend the Dungeon — Our Score: 8.0/10

A humble skeleton soldier serves the Lady Succubus loyally — until they're both killed. He's then granted the ability to return to the beginning of any timeline in which he dies, slowly accumulating skills and power across each loop. It's a dark fantasy Groundhog Day, and it works far better than the premise suggests.

**Why it's great:** The MC's growth from literal monster fodder to a force capable of reshaping timelines is methodical and earned. The tone is darker than most on this list, which gives the emotional payoffs real weight.

[Read on MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/title/d993f789-e7e5-4832-92fd-37614220b427)
