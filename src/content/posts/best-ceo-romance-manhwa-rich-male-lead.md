## Why CEO Romance Manhwa Hits Different

Korean manhwa has turned the wealthy-businessman love story into an art form. Whether it is the enemies-to-lovers tension of an office blind date or the slow burn of falling for a boss who cannot admit his feelings, these series deliver sharp power dynamics alongside genuine emotional depth. Korean work culture — long hours, rigid hierarchies — lends authentic weight to the office setting that sets these titles apart from Western rom-coms. Our list spans both modern CEO stories and historical nobleman variants; both tropes scratch the same rich-male-lead itch.

Our scores are editorial opinions, not aggregated ratings.

## Rankings

### 1. A Business Proposal

**Our score: 9.2 / 10**

The gold standard of the office CEO romance genre. The female lead stumbles into a blind date arranged for her friend and comes face to face with her actual CEO, Taemu Kang — cold, impossibly composed, and immediately suspicious of her motives. What follows is an enemies-to-lovers spiral where both parties try to sabotage the arrangement while growing undeniably attached. The series spawned a live-action drama hit across the region and remains one of the most re-read romance manhwa of its era.

**Why it's great:** Perfect comedic timing, a CEO male lead who earns every beat of his thaw, and a female lead who is genuinely funny rather than passive. The pacing never drags.

[Read on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=A+Business+Proposal)

### 2. See You in My 19th Life

**Our score: 8.8 / 10**

Jieum Ban carries the memories of all eighteen of her past lives — and in her nineteenth, she is determined to find the boy she loved before a tragic accident cut her story short. He is now a quietly wealthy, emotionally guarded man who cannot explain why this stranger feels so familiar. With over 97 million Webtoon views and a Completed label, this is one of the most celebrated romance series on the platform.

**Why it's great:** The male lead's wealth is incidental to who he is. It is his emotional walls — and the way Jieum methodically dismantles them — that make the story magnetic.

[Read on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=See+You+in+My+19th+Life)

### 3. The Villainess Turns the Hourglass

**Our score: 8.6 / 10**

After Aria's mother marries into nobility, she lives lavishly — until her scheming stepsister engineers her death. Armed with a magical hourglass, Aria rewinds time and rebuilds herself from a naïve socialite into a calculating survivor. The male lead is a wealthy, powerful noble — cold and composed in exactly the way the genre demands. Completed, so you can binge the entire arc without waiting on updates.

**Why it's great:** One of the finest protagonist evolution arcs in the villainess subgenre. The slow-burn romance is worth every chapter of patience, and completed status makes this an ideal binge read.

[Read on MangaDex →](https://mangadex.org/title/73bc69fa-9ba9-4533-a243-ebc11651339f)

### 4. This Villainess Wants a Divorce!

**Our score: 8.2 / 10**

Canaria wakes up reincarnated as the villain of a novel — and the villain's fate is execution by her own husband. Her strategy: be so harmless and agreeable that she earns her divorce before the ending catches up with her. What she does not account for is a husband who is far more complex than the story made him out to be. Completed, with a satisfying relationship arc that earns its finale.

**Why it's great:** The male lead has genuine depth — powerful and privileged, but never just a set piece. His evolution alongside Canaria is what separates this from the average villainess escape story.

[Read on MangaDex →](https://mangadex.org/title/7e020929-65a7-4ece-905a-7e63c52709f8)

### 5. His Devilish New Hire

**Our score: 8.0 / 10**

A boss-and-new-employee setup that leans fully into every tension the title promises. The CEO male lead is demanding, infuriatingly competent, and — once his attention locks onto the female lead — unflinching in his interest. The workplace setting keeps the power dynamic front and center without letting it overwhelm the emotional beats. Published in 2020 and a frequent recommendation alongside A Business Proposal for fans of the same trope.

**Why it's great:** If you want the cold-CEO-who-thaws-for-one-person dynamic delivered cleanly and without fuss, this is one of the most efficient executions of that formula.

[Read on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=His+Devilish+New+Hire)

### 6. Can't Get Enough of You

**Our score: 7.9 / 10**

A 2023 entry that arrived with strong early reader enthusiasm. The CEO male lead is wealthy, attentive, and the kind of romantic who commits completely once he has fallen — which makes the will-they-won't-they tension all the more effective. Contemporary art style and punchy pacing make this one easy to binge through in a single sitting.

**Why it's great:** Refreshingly direct about its romance beats — no unnecessary filler drama, just well-executed chemistry in a polished modern package that feels current rather than formulaic.

[Read on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=Can%27t+Get+Enough+of+You)

### 7. CEO's Top Secret

**Our score: 7.8 / 10**

Ha Yunseo is a romance author publishing under the pen name Red Cherry — and her path crosses a CEO who carries far more layers than his polished exterior suggests. Both leads have secrets, and watching them misread each other before the truth finally lands is the central pleasure of the series. The meta angle — a romance writer caught inside her own love story — gives it a self-aware charm that lifts it above the standard office romance formula.

**Why it's great:** Smart enough to subvert its own tropes while still delivering everything the genre promises. A strong pick for readers who want wit alongside warmth.

[Read on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=CEO%27s+Top+Secret)

### 8. The Wicked Wife of a Scheming CEO

**Our score: 7.6 / 10**

Married into a powerful CEO's world under circumstances neither party fully chose, the female lead must navigate a relationship built on schemes rather than warmth — and a husband whose motives are never quite what they seem. The push-pull between two sharp, stubborn characters drives the tension, and the story rewards readers who prefer watching leads earn each other's trust over easy romance.

**Why it's great:** Neither lead is a pushover. That mutual competence makes the dynamic far more interesting than a straightforward power-imbalance setup. A good follow-up for fans of The Villainess Turns the Hourglass who want the scheming energy transplanted into a fully modern setting.

[Read on Webtoon →](https://www.webtoons.com/en/search?keyword=The+Wicked+Wife+of+a+Scheming+CEO)
