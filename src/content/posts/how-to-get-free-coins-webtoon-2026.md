## Why WEBTOON Coins Disappear So Fast

If you've ever hit a cliffhanger and had to wait—or pay—to keep reading, you already know the WEBTOON coin problem. Coins are the platform's premium currency, used to unlock paid episodes of Originals series before they become free. The good news: WEBTOON officially supports several ways to earn coins without spending real money, and in 2026 those methods are better than ever.

This guide covers every legitimate, in-app method that works today—no shady generators, no hacks, just the real stuff that WEBTOON actually sanctions.

## Method 1: Coin Hunter (Your Best Free Option)

Coin Hunter—sometimes called Treasure Hunt—is WEBTOON's built-in rewards hub. Available in the US and select regions, it lets you earn coins by completing short tasks: answering surveys, trying out partner games, or filling out quick questionnaires. Tasks refresh regularly, so checking in daily adds up fast.

**How to access it:** Open the WEBTOON app → tap the Coin icon → look for the Coin Hunter or Treasure Hunt banner. Individual tasks are usually worth 1–3 coins each, but they're completely free and backed by WEBTOON directly.

## Method 2: Official Reading Events and Marathons

WEBTOON periodically runs reading challenges where completing a set number of chapters—usually from CANVAS or Originals series—rewards you with free coins. These events are announced on the app's home screen and on WEBTOON's official social media accounts.

Keep notifications on so you don't miss a limited-time window. Event coins are deposited directly into your account once you hit the chapter goal—no extra steps required.

## Method 3: Daily Pass Episode Rebates

During special rebate events, unlocking certain Daily Pass episodes earns you bonus coins back—up to 15 coins per qualifying unlock, according to documented guides on the method. This turns your episode unlocks into a partial coin refund, making it one of the smartest ways to stack coins if you're already reading Daily Pass series.

Watch the app's event banners to confirm a rebate window is active *before* you unlock anything—timing matters.

## Method 4: Official Promo Codes

WEBTOON releases official promo codes through its social media channels, newsletters, and special partnerships. Redeeming them is straightforward:

- Visit **webtoons.com** in any browser. iOS users must use the website rather than the app due to Apple's in-app purchase rules.
- Log in to your account.
- Navigate to **Buy Coins → Redeem Free Coins**, enter the code, and confirm.
- Coins appear in both the app and website instantly.

Codes are region- and language-specific—if one doesn't work, check whether it has expired or is targeted at a different region. Following WEBTOON's official social media accounts is the most reliable way to catch new codes as they drop.

## Method 5: Use the Daily Pass System Strategically

The Daily Pass isn't a coin-earning method, but it's the best way to *avoid spending coins* in the first place. Many Originals series offer one free episode per day via Daily Pass. For series you're not binge-reading right now, stacking Daily Pass unlocks over time lets you read for free with patience—saving your earned coins for newer or premium-only titles.

## Quick Tips to Maximize Your Balance

- **Check Coin Hunter every day**—tasks reset and small amounts compound over a week.
- **Follow WEBTOON's official social accounts** for surprise promo code drops tied to anniversaries and collaborations.
- **Wait for a rebate event** before unlocking Daily Pass episodes—don't burn unlocks outside the rebate window.
- **Watch for official coin sales**—WEBTOON occasionally runs promotions that give you more coins per dollar if you do choose to purchase.
