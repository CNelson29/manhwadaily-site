## Introduction

Tower of God redefined what manhwa could be — sprawling world-building, jaw-dropping power systems, and a story that makes you feel every betrayal and triumph in your bones. If you've caught up and are desperately hunting for that same high, this list is your salvation. Whether it's dungeon-crawling survival, floor-by-floor ascension, or gate-breaking chaos, these titles deliver the goods. Let's dive in!

## The Rankings

### 1. Solo Leveling

The manhwa that introduced millions of Western readers to Korean webtoons, Solo Leveling follows Sung Jinwoo, the world's weakest hunter, who gains a mysterious system that lets him level up without limit inside deadly gates and dungeons. The art by Dubu (REDICE Studio) is some of the most polished in the medium — every shadow monarch power-up is a visual feast. With 179 chapters of relentless escalation, this is the gold standard of dungeon manhwa.

**Why it's great:** The power fantasy is perfectly paced, and the lore surrounding the Monarchs and Rulers adds genuine depth to what could've been a shallow grind story.

**Rating: 9.5/10**

### 2. Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint

Kim Dokja is the sole reader of a web novel called Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse — and then the novel's scenario becomes reality. Using his encyclopedic knowledge of the story's events, he navigates a brutal world of scenarios and constellations who watch humanity like gods watching gladiators. This epic runs over 150 chapters with consistently stunning art that evolves dramatically as the stakes rise.

**Why it's great:** The meta-narrative angle is genuinely clever, and the emotional payoff for long-time readers is extraordinary — few manhwas make you cry this hard.

**Rating: 9.3/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Omniscient+Reader%27s+Viewpoint) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Omniscient+Reader%27s+Viewpoint) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Omniscient+Reader%27s+Viewpoint)

### 3. The Beginning After the End

King Grey, the most powerful mage-warrior in his world, dies and is reborn as Arthur Leywin in a fantasy realm filled with dungeons, beasts, and ancient threats. What starts as a charming cultivation story deepens into a massive conflict involving two warring worlds, with over 170 chapters of increasingly intense action and lore. The art style is clean and cinematic, particularly during dungeon-dive sequences.

**Why it's great:** The slow-burn character growth and rich magic system make this feel like a proper fantasy novel given stunning visual form.

**Rating: 9.0/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=The+Beginning+After+the+End) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=The+Beginning+After+the+End) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=The+Beginning+After+the+End)

### 4. Dungeon Reset

Jung Dawon is an ordinary guy trapped in a dungeon game — except when the dungeon resets each day, he's inexplicably left behind and slowly gains access to crafting skills no other player has. It's a refreshingly creative take on the dungeon-survival genre, blending survival crafting with comedy and genuine tension over 200+ chapters. The protagonist's ingenuity is endlessly entertaining to watch.

**Why it's great:** The crafting system is wildly inventive and the humor hits just right, making this one of the most unique dungeon manhwas on this list.

**Rating: 8.6/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Dungeon+Reset) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Dungeon+Reset) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Dungeon+Reset)

### 5. Second Life Ranker

Yeon-woo discovers his twin brother's hidden diary revealing he was murdered inside a massive tower called the Obelisk. He enters the same tower, using his brother's notes as a roadmap to climb floors, build power, and exact revenge on those who betrayed his sibling. The revenge plot gives every floor-clear an extra layer of emotional weight across its 150+ chapter run.

**Why it's great:** The tower structure directly mirrors Tower of God's floor-climbing formula, and the vengeful protagonist with a dead-man's guide is a phenomenal hook.

**Rating: 8.8/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Second+Life+Ranker) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Second+Life+Ranker) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Second+Life+Ranker)

### 6. Hardcore Leveling Warrior

Once the number-one player in the virtual MMORPG Lucid Adventure, our cocky hero is suddenly reset to Level 1 by a mysterious villain and must claw his way back to the top. Mixing dungeon-crawler energy with MMORPG mechanics and genuine character development, this Webtoon original ran for over 330 episodes. The art has a stylized boldness that makes fight scenes absolutely pop off the screen.

**Why it's great:** The gaming mechanics layered over a surprisingly emotional story about identity and redemption make this one of Webtoon's most underrated epics.

**Rating: 8.5/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Hardcore+Leveling+Warrior) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Hardcore+Leveling+Warrior) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Hardcore+Leveling+Warrior)

🛒 [Get the Official English Volume on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Hardcore+Leveling+Warrior+manga+volume+1&tag=manhwadaily-20)

### 7. Eleceed

A kind-hearted boy with electric speed powers teams up with a world-class martial artist hiding inside a fluffy cat — and together they're drawn into a secret world of awakened fighters vying for supremacy. By the same author as Noblesse, Eleceed has a warm, charming tone balanced with seriously hype tournament and dungeon-adjacent combat arcs across 260+ chapters. The character dynamics are genuinely delightful.

**Why it's great:** It's the perfect palette cleanser between darker tower manhwas — the humor is excellent, but it never lets the action slack off.

**Rating: 8.4/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Eleceed) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Eleceed) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Eleceed)

🛒 [Get the Official English Volume on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Eleceed+manga+volume+1&tag=manhwadaily-20)

### 8. Overgeared

Grid, a talentless loner in the virtual reality MMORPG Satisfy, stumbles upon a legendary blacksmith class and slowly forges himself — literally and figuratively — into the most powerful player in the world. With over 190 chapters, Overgeared features intricate item-crafting mechanics, massive raid dungeons, and a protagonist whose growth from pathetic loser to genuine hero is one of manhwa's most satisfying arcs. The dungeon-raid chapters are ferociously well-drawn.

**Why it's great:** The depth of the game mechanics rivals actual RPGs, and Grid's underdog journey earns every power-up in a way few protagonists do.

**Rating: 8.7/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Overgeared) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Overgeared) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Overgeared)

### 9. The Tutorial Is Too Hard

A top-tier gamer selects Hell difficulty on a tutorial that's supposed to be a formality — and gets trapped in an impossibly brutal tower of trials that no one else on Earth can survive. Combining dark survival horror vibes with strategic combat and a surprisingly rich cast of side characters, this one keeps the tension sky-high throughout its 180+ chapters. The isolation of the protagonist makes every small victory feel monumental.

**Why it's great:** The sheer creativity of the trial designs and the protagonist's problem-solving under extreme pressure make each chapter feel like a genuine puzzle thriller.

**Rating: 8.6/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=The+Tutorial+Is+Too+Hard) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=The+Tutorial+Is+Too+Hard) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=The+Tutorial+Is+Too+Hard)

🛒 [Get the Official English Volume on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Tutorial+Is+Too+Hard+manga+volume+1&tag=manhwadaily-20)

### 10. Kill the Hero

In a world invaded by dungeons and monsters, Kim Woo-jin is betrayed and killed by the hero he helped rise to power — only to reawaken with memories of his past life and a singular mission: destroy the false hero before he can enslave humanity. The dark, gritty art style perfectly matches the cold, methodical revenge narrative across 130+ chapters. Every dungeon conquest doubles as a chess move in a larger revenge plot.

**Why it's great:** The anti-hero protagonist and the political manipulation layered into dungeon raids give this a smart thriller edge most power-fantasy manhwas skip entirely.

**Rating: 8.3/10**

**Where to read:**[Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/search?keyword=Kill+the+Hero) · [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/search?q=Kill+the+Hero) · [Tapas](https://tapas.io/search?q=Kill+the+Hero)
